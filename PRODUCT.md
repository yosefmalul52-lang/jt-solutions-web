# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Israeli small-to-mid business owners and operators (Hebrew RTL) evaluating a digital partner. They arrive from referrals, search, or paid campaigns — often on mobile — with limited patience for jargon. They need to understand quickly whether JT Solutions can turn their marketing spend into organized, trackable leads.

## Product Purpose

JT Solutions is a full-stack digital agency site that converts visitors into diagnostic-call leads. It presents services (landing pages, business websites, eCommerce, branding, campaigns, automation), builds trust through case studies and process clarity, and funnels users to a contact/diagnostic form. Success = qualified leads who book a ~15-minute strategy call.

## Brand Personality

**Confident · Expert · Direct**

Benefit-driven Hebrew copy. Non-technical language. Speaks as a single accountable partner (Yosef Malul, end-to-end), not a faceless vendor. Premium Israeli B2B tone — professional, trustworthy, results-oriented without hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates (cream backgrounds, hero-metric cards, gradient text, identical icon-card grids)
- Flashy agency sites (heavy neon, gratuitous animation, over-designed motion for its own sake)
- Cheap template look (stock-icon clutter, vague copy, section scaffolding by reflex)
- Full AI-slop spectrum: tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section, numbered 01/02/03 markers without real sequence, glassmorphism-as-default, side-stripe accent borders

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through clarity** — Every section answers a business question (what you get, how it works, proof it works). No decorative filler.
2. **One path to action** — Primary conversion is the free diagnostic call; secondary paths (WhatsApp, portfolio) support, not compete.
3. **Practice what you preach** — The site itself demonstrates conversion craft: fast load, clear hierarchy, measured motion, accessible forms.
4. **Expert confidence, not noise** — Motion and visuals reinforce the funnel story; they never gate content or slow the user down.
5. **Hebrew-first, mobile-real** — RTL, readable contrast, and layouts tested at real breakpoints; most visitors won't be on a designer's monitor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: sufficient contrast on body and placeholder text, keyboard-navigable forms and menus, semantic headings, reduced-motion alternatives for all animations. EqualWeb widget remains as an additional layer.
