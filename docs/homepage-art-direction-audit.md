## Top 3 weakest sections

1. **Bento / Deliverables (6.0)** — Not on page; feels like generic service grid when present.
2. **Process (6.4)** — Not on page; rainbow timeline reads template-like.
3. **Trust chips (6.8)** — Functional but flat; no studio personality.

---

## Top 3 conversion-critical sections

1. **Hero (7.8)** — First impression + primary CTA.
2. **Packages (6.8)** — Pathway selection before contact.
3. **Final CTA (7.4)** — Form submission close.

---

## Generic / repetitive patterns

- Alternating white/soft-blue without section-specific character.
- Section headers all use same eyebrow + H2 + subline rhythm (good) but similar visual weight.
- Cards across Identification, Pricing, FAQ share same border/shadow language — needs differentiation per role.
- Multi-color accents (violet, cyan, green) dilute blue/navy brand focus.
- Bento + Process missing from funnel — story jumps Solution → Projects → Pricing.

---

## Missing depth / "wow"

- Hero: orbit visual strong but background lacks dimensional mesh presence.
- Identification: central card not dominant enough vs side pains.
- Solution: hub is strong; section entry could feel more "ordered system."
- Carousel: good direction; needs to feel like portfolio centerpiece.
- No deliverables "productized" moment between solution and proof.
- No process methodology between proof and pricing.

---

## Must NOT break

- Problem Floating Zigzag Journey (no spine revert).
- Solution hub canvas (dark system map).
- Carousel: image-only, no overlay/text on images, coverflow with z-depth.
- Copy, API, form schema, routes.
- Reduced-motion paths.

---

## Focused upgrade plan

1. **Add** Deliverables + Process to homepage funnel (Solution → Deliverables → Projects → Process → Pricing).
2. **Hero** — stronger mesh, orbit glow, trust strip refinement.
3. **Identification** — pain index labels, amplified central card.
4. **Problem** — stronger break-step visual weight (CSS only).
5. **Solution** — brand-blue accent alignment, section atmosphere.
6. **Bento** — category badges, featured hierarchy, productized layout.
7. **Carousel** — active-card glow, section centerpiece framing.
8. **Process** — blue/navy timeline, larger step numbers.
9. **Packages** — pathway fit tags, featured elevation.
10. **FAQ + CTA** — lighter visual weight, stronger close framing.
